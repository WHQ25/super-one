## What it does

Long-running work keeps going while you do something else, and tells you when it is done — including on your phone, if you have left the desk.

## How it works

- A backgrounded task is tracked by the harness. When it finishes, the agent is re-invoked with the result rather than having to poll for it.
- Notifications route through the same relay path as everything else, so a task that completes while you are away arrives on the paired device.
- Parking a session does not stop its stream. A session you moved out of the way keeps producing; mounted sessions are exempted from teardown precisely so that "I'll look at this later" does not mean "I killed it".

## Why polling is the wrong instinct

If the harness re-invokes you when tracked work finishes, a short-interval check is wasted — it wakes up, finds nothing new, and goes back to sleep, having spent a turn. The exception is work the harness cannot see: a CI run, a deploy, a remote queue. There, an interval matched to how fast that state actually changes is the right tool, and a long fallback covers the case where the notification never arrives at all.

## FAQ

**Do I lose the output if I close the panel?** No — the task's output lands in the session's transcript, which is where you left it.
