## What it does

Several phones can watch the same desktop session at once — your phone and your tablet both following the run, both able to reply.

## How it works

- Each device subscribes to **one** session at a time. That is the unit: a device is looking at a conversation, not at the whole desktop.
- The relay buffers events for devices that are not currently connected, and buffer GC runs on **per-device ACK** rather than on a timer. An offline device counts as pending, not as gone — otherwise the phone in your pocket comes back to a hole in the transcript.
- Event payloads are trimmed for mobile, which is where a new tool type has to be explicitly exempted. A tool the trimmer does not know about is a tool whose output silently disappears on the phone while looking fine on the desktop.

## FAQ

**Do the phones see each other?** They see the same session; there is no separate presence surface between them.

**What happens if two phones reply at once?** They queue into the same session, in the order the relay receives them.
