## What it does

The relay is a Cloudflare Worker backed by Durable Objects. You can run it on your own Cloudflare account instead of using the hosted one.

## How it works

- One Durable Object per room holds the connection state and the event buffer for the devices attached to it. Durable Objects are the right shape here because a room needs a single consistent place to serialise events — the alternative is reconciling ordering across edge locations.
- Buffered events are released on per-device ACK, so a device that goes offline gets its backlog rather than a gap.
- Deployment is `wrangler deploy` against your account; the desktop then points at your Worker's URL.
- The relay ships on its own semver tag, separate from the desktop app's release train, because the two do not have to move together.

## Why this is worth offering

The relay never sees plaintext, so self-hosting is not a security upgrade — it is an availability and jurisdiction choice. Some teams need the traffic to transit infrastructure they control, and that is a legitimate requirement even when the contents are already opaque. Being able to say "run your own, here is how" is cheaper than arguing about it.

## FAQ

**Does self-hosting weaken anything?** No; the encryption is unchanged. You are swapping who operates the router.

**What does it cost?** Workers and Durable Objects pricing on your own account — the relay does very little compute per message.
