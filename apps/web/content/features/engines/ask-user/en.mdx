## What it does

When the agent needs a decision from you, it asks with structured options rather than stopping with a paragraph of prose and waiting.

## How it works

- The question renders as a card with the options as buttons, so answering is a click rather than typing a sentence the agent then has to parse.
- Multiple questions can arrive together, each with its own set of choices, and multi-select is supported where the choices are not exclusive.
- Options can carry a preview, so a question about two layouts shows both instead of describing them.
- Your selection is echoed inline in the result, so the transcript records what you actually chose rather than just that you answered.

## Why structured beats free text

An open question invites an answer the agent has to interpret, and interpretation is where the misunderstanding happens. Presenting the choices it can actually act on removes that step — and forces the agent to have thought through what the alternatives are before asking.

## FAQ

**Can I answer something other than the options?** Yes — there is always a free-text path for when none of the options is right.
