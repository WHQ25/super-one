## What it does

Change a session's engine while it is running, without starting over somewhere else.

## What carries over and what does not

The working directory, the project and the file context carry over. Conversation memory does not — each harness owns its own transcript format, and pretending otherwise would produce a history the new engine cannot faithfully replay.

That constraint is why handoff exists alongside the switch: it writes a durable Markdown brief so the plan and the constraints travel even though the raw conversation cannot.

## Why you would

- The remaining work suits a different engine — long reasoning on one, fast focused edits on another.
- The current engine lacks a capability the task now needs.
- One account hit a rate limit and the work should continue rather than wait.

## What stays independent

Memory, slash commands and tool permissions are per harness, so switching does not carry one engine's instructions into another where they may not apply.

## FAQ

**Does the whole project switch?** No. The engine is a property of the session, so other sessions in the same project keep theirs.
