## What it does

Pick a hue, and the entire app retints. Pick a different one per harness, and you'll always know whether you're talking to Claude or Codex without reading a label.

## How it works

- Every colour token in the app derives from a single `--brand-hue` variable using OKLch math.
- The hue picker lives in the header. Drag the slider, watch the app reflow live.
- Each harness ships with a default hue (warm orange for Claude, cool green for Codex), but anything is fair game.
- Works in both light and dark mode — saturation and lightness stops are matched per mode.

## Why it matters

- Visual cue prevents the "wait, which agent did I just ask to delete that?" moment.
- Personal — make the workspace match how you feel about today.
- Accessible — high-contrast palettes don't require manual overrides; the token system does the math.

## FAQ

**Will my hue sync across devices?** Currently per-device. Multi-device sync is on the roadmap.
